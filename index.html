POST-QUANTUM CRYPTOGRAPHY

Standards matter. So does implementation.

KAPTOR’s cryptographic engineering focus includes hardware acceleration for post-quantum algorithms. Algorithm selection is only one part of a secure implementation: protocol design, key management, interfaces and validation matter just as much.

Standards status checked 6 November 2026. FIPS 203, 204 and 205 are final NIST standards. FN-DSA/Falcon is still in the NIST standardisation process; it is not represented here as a final FIPS standard. See the NIST PQC publications register.

Reference to a NIST standard describes the algorithm standard, not a certification or validation of KAPTOR hardware, firmware or a deployed product. Product-specific conformance and performance require evidence from the relevant implementation and test configuration.
