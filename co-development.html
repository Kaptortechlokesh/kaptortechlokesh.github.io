DEFENCE SYSTEMS / SPACE / DEEP TECH

OPEN TO APPLICATIONS

Bring the requirement.
Let’s define the product.

We welcome introductions from established companies, emerging technology firms, system integrators, manufacturers and specialist engineering teams working in defence, space and adjacent deep-tech sectors.

The starting point can be a defined technical requirement, a platform integration challenge, a subsystem that needs to be developed or an early product concept that needs engineering definition.

An application is an invitation to discuss fit. It does not imply a partnership, contract, funding commitment or acceptance into a programme.

POSSIBLE JOINT WORK

What we can explore together.

The scope is defined around the partner’s mission, constraints and ownership model. These are areas for discussion, not claims that each product is already on the market.

01 / EMBEDDED

Secure electronics & embedded systems

Controllers, secure subsystems, hardware interfaces and electronics for constrained or mission-critical platforms.

02 / AEROSPACE

Avionics & space subsystems

On-board electronics, data handling, interface modules and platform-specific subsystem development.

03 / COMMUNICATIONS

Communications & data links

Hardware concepts for RF, optical and wired links, gateways, telemetry and command pathways.

04 / DEFENCE TECH

Rugged & field-deployable systems

Enclosures, electronic assemblies and edge hardware shaped by environmental and integration requirements.

05 / COMPUTE

Edge compute & mission processing

Embedded compute, processing modules and specialised electronics for distributed operational systems.

06 / NEW PRODUCTS

Other deep-tech product ideas

Bring a clear problem statement in an adjacent domain. We can assess whether there is a meaningful engineering fit.

A PRACTICAL STARTING POINT

From first conversation
to an engineering scope.

A useful first exchange is specific and bounded. We would rather understand the real interface, environment and constraint than start with a broad promise.

01

Introduce

Share your organisation, the mission area and the problem you are trying to solve.

02

Understand

Discuss platform context, interfaces, constraints, maturity and intended users.

03

Scope

Identify a realistic work package, responsibilities, dependencies and evaluation criteria.

04

Decide

Agree whether to proceed to a technical workshop, proposal or a defined next step.
